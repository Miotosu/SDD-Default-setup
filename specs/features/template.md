# Feature: <Name>

> Fill in every section. Empty sections fail Refiner review.

## Intent (Why / Who)
- **Problem:** What user / business pain does this solve?
- **Audience:** Who interacts with it, in what role?
- **Out of scope:** What this feature explicitly does *not* do.

## Behavior (How / When)
- **Triggers:** Events, inputs, or schedules that initiate the feature.
- **Logic flow:** Step-by-step description of the happy path.
- **State transitions:** Allowed states and the events that move between them.

## Boundaries (Input / Output / Constraints)
- **Inputs:** Sources, schemas, validation rules.
- **Outputs:** Sinks, schemas, side effects.
- **Constraints:** Performance, scale, regulatory, or compatibility limits.

## Safety (Access / Prohibited States)
- **Access control:** Who can invoke, who can read, who can mutate.
- **Prohibited states:** Combinations or transitions that must never occur.
- **Audit trail:** What must be logged, retained, and where.

## Failure Modes (Errors / Resilience)
- **Expected errors:** Known failure cases and the user-visible result for each.
- **Recovery:** Retries, fallbacks, manual remediation steps.
- **Observability:** Metrics, alerts, and dashboards required to detect failure.
